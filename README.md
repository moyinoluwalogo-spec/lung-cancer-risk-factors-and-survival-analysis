# Lung Cancer Risk Factor & Survival Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a lung cancer risk factor and survival dataset to identify patterns between patient demographics, lifestyle factors, cancer stages, cancer types, and survival outcomes.

The project demonstrates my ability to clean, analyze, visualize, and communicate insights from a healthcare dataset using Microsoft Excel.

## Problem Statement

Lung cancer outcomes can be influenced by factors such as age, smoking status, family history, and cancer stage.

The goal of this analysis is to explore these factors and identify meaningful patterns in survival outcomes.

## Project Objectives

* Analyze patient demographics.
* Examine the distribution of lung cancer types.
* Analyze survival outcomes by cancer stage.
* Compare survival outcomes across smoking statuses.
* Explore survival outcomes by age group and gender.
* Identify important patterns in the dataset.
* Present findings through an interactive Excel dashboard.

## Key Findings

### Cancer Type

NSCLC (Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer) was the most common cancer type in the dataset, while SCLC (Small Cell Lung Cancer) represented a smaller proportion.

### Survival Outcome

The dataset contained:

* **756 patients with a positive survival outcome**
* **1,244 patients with a negative survival outcome**

### Cancer Stage

The analysis showed differences in survival outcomes across cancer stages, with Stage 1 having the highest number of positive survival outcomes and later stages showing fewer positive outcomes.

### Smoking Status

Survival outcomes were compared across current smokers, former smokers, and never smokers to identify differences between the groups.

### Age Group

The analysis examined patients across age groups ranging from **30–39 to 80–89 years**.
